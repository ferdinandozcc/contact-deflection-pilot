# Policy base (public sources only)

Built from Groupon's public help centre and terms, mainly the UK/EMEA pages. Checked 25 Nov 2026.
This is my reading of public pages, not Groupon's internal policy. Each rule has an ID that the agent cites in its answer and the eval checks.

| ID | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| P-LOCAL-CANCEL | Local deal: you can cancel within 14 calendar days of the confirmation email if the voucher is unredeemed and no appointment is booked. The refund goes to the original payment method. | groupon.co.uk/legal/termsOfSaleLocal (5.1); UK help centre "Refund Policy for Local Deals (UK)" |
| P-PAYMENT-CHANGED | If the original payment method has been cancelled, has expired or has changed, the customer must tell support. The agent can't change where money goes. | groupon.co.uk/legal/termsOfSaleLocal |
| P-EXPIRED | Once expired, a voucher can't be redeemed and is generally non-refundable. A customer who believes it expired because of exceptional circumstances can ask support to review the case, but a refund isn't guaranteed. | groupon.co.uk/faq/changes-refunds/expired-groupon-policy |
| P-ALREADY-REDEEMED | Voucher shows as redeemed but wasn't used: support must review it as soon as possible. | groupon.co.uk/faq/using-your-groupon/troubleshooting-groupon-voucher-redemption-issues |

## Escalation rules (mine, not Groupon's)

Some cases always go to a person, whatever the intent:
- **Legal or regulatory:** chargeback, lawyer, small claims, trading standards, CMA.
- **Possible fraud or account compromise.**
- **Vulnerable customer:** bereavement or medical emergency.
- **Repeat contact:** two or more earlier contacts on the same order. If the customer is back, the first answer didn't resolve it.
- **Low confidence:** no clear intent, or more than one.
